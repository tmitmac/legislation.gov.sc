TITLE: Extended Wartime Measures Act Repeal Act 2003

ACTNUMBER: 2003 No 98

TYPE: Act

STATUS: In Force

VERSION: latest

AGENCY: His Majesty's Treasury of Square Country

DATEOFASSENT: 17 October 2003

COMMENCEMENT: see section 2

PARTNUMBER: 1

PARTTITLE: Repeal of Act

SECTION: 1

HEADING: Short Title

This Act is the Extended Wartime Measures Repeal Act 2003.

SECTION: 2

HEADING: Commencement

This Act comes into force the day after it receives Royal assent.

SECTION: 3

HEADING: Extended Wartime Measures Act repealed

The Extended Wartime Measures Act (1915 No 62) is repealed.
