TITLE: Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries, and Natural Resources Establishment Act 2026

ACTNUMBER: 2026 No 03

TYPE: Act

STATUS: not yet in force

VERSION: Latest

AGENCY: Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries, and Natural Resources

DATEOFASSENT: 25 July 2026

COMMENCEMENT: see section 2

PARTNUMBER: 1

PARTTITLE: Preliminary Provisions

SECTION: 1

HEADING: Short Title

This Act is the **Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries, and Natural Resources Establishment Act 2026**.

SECTION: 2

HEADING: Commencement

1. This Act comes into force on 1 September 2026.

SECTION: 3

HEADING: Purpose

The purpose of this Act is to—
(a) merge the Ministry of Agriculture, the Ministry of Fisheries, and the Ministry for Natural Resources into a single ministry; and
(b) establish the Ministry for Agriculture, Fisheries, and Natural Resources; and
(c) ensure continuity of governmental administration during the transition; and
(d) provide for the transfer of employees, assets, liabilities, statutory functions, and legal proceedings; and
(e) amend and repeal enactments rendered obsolete by the merger.

SECTION: 4

HEADING: Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—
**former ministries** means—
(a) the Ministry of Agriculture; and
(b) the Ministry of Fisheries; and
(c) the Ministry for Natural Resources.
**Minister** means the Minister responsible for the New Ministry.
**New Ministry** means the Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries, and Natural Resources established under [[section:5]].
**Parliament** means His Majesty's Parliament of Square Country.
**Public Service Commissioner** means the person given the powers of the Public Service Commissioner under the [[Act:Public Service Act 1902]].

PARTNUMBER: 2

PARTTITLE: Establishment of Ministry

SECTION: 5

HEADING: Establishment of Ministry for Agriculture, Fisheries, and Natural Resources

1. On and after the commencement of this Act, there is established a department of State to be known as the Ministry for Agriculture, Fisheries, and Natural Resources.
2. The New Ministry is responsible for all functions, powers, and duties previously exercised by any of the former ministries.
3. The New Ministry is under the direction of the Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries, and Natural Resources.
4. The New Ministry may establish internal divisions, directorates, or units for the purpose of administering its functions, including (without limitation) divisions for—
(a) agriculture and rural development;
(b) fisheries management and marine resources;
(c) natural resources stewardship;
(d) environmental compliance and monitoring;
(e) scientific and technical services.

SECTION: 6

HEADING: Dissolution of former ministries

1. On and after the commencement of this Act, the former ministries are dissolved.
2. All functions, powers, and duties of the former ministries are transferred to the New Ministry.
3. Any reference in any enactment, regulation, instrument, contract, or document to a former ministry must, unless the context otherwise requires, be read as a reference to the New Ministry.
4. The Governor-General may, by Order in Council, declare that any reference to a former ministry in a specified enactment or instrument is to be read as a reference to a specified division of the New Ministry.

SECTION: 7

HEADING: Transfer of employees

1. Every person employed by a former ministry immediately before the commencement of this Act becomes an employee of the New Ministry on that date.
2. The transfer does not—
(a) break the employee’s continuity of employment; or
(b) affect any entitlement to salary, leave, or other employment conditions; or
(c) require the employee’s consent.
3. The Public Service Commissioner may issue directions relating to the integration of staff structures, grading, and remuneration.

SECTION: 8

HEADING: Transfer of assets and liabilities

1. All assets, liabilities, rights, and obligations of the former ministries vest in the New Ministry on the commencement of this Act.
2. Any legal proceedings commenced by or against a former ministry may be continued, completed, or enforced by or against the New Ministry.
3. Any permit, licence, approval, or authorisation issued by a former ministry remains valid until its expiry or replacement.

SECTION: 9

HEADING: Transfer of statutory powers

1. Any statutory power conferred on the head of a former ministry is, on and after the commencement of this Act, exercisable by the Secretary of the New Ministry.
2. Any delegation made by a former ministry continues in force until revoked or replaced by the New Ministry.

SECTION: 10

HEADING: Records and information

1. All records, data, archives, and information held by a former ministry become records of the New Ministry.
2. The New Ministry must ensure continuity of access to records required for ongoing regulatory, enforcement, or administrative functions.

SECTION: 11

HEADING: Existing contracts and agreements

All contracts, memoranda of understanding, international agreements, and cooperative arrangements entered into by a former ministry continue in force as if entered into by the New Ministry.

PARTNUMBER: 3

PARTTITLE: Transitional, Savings, and Miscellaneous Provisions

SECTION: 12

HEADING: Transitional Secretariat

1. A Transitional Secretariat is established for the period beginning on the day after Royal Assent and ending on 30 June 2027.
2. The Secretariat is responsible for—
(a) coordinating the merger;
(b) aligning regulatory frameworks;
(c) preparing consolidated operational policies;
(d)advising the Minister on transitional matters.
3. The Secretariat must report quarterly to the Minister.

SECTION: 13

HEADING: Transitional regulations

The Governor-General may, by Order in Council, make regulations for the purpose of facilitating the transition from the former ministries to the New Ministry, including regulations that—
(a) modify or suspend provisions of any enactment for a period not exceeding 12 months;
(b) provide for temporary administrative arrangements;
(c) validate anything done in anticipation of the commencement of this Act.

SECTION: 14

HEADING: Savings provisions

Nothing in this Act affects—
(a) any investigation commenced by a former ministry;
(b) any enforcement action underway;
(c) any obligation imposed on any person under an enactment administered by a former ministry.

SECTION: 15

HEADING: Consequential amendments

The Acts listed in **Schedule 1** are amended as set out in that Schedule.

SECTION: 16

HEADING: Repeals

The enactments listed in **Schedule 2** are repealed.

SECTION: 17

HEADING: Review of operation of Act

1. The Minister must, within 3 years after the commencement of this Act, cause a review of the operation and effectiveness of this Act.
2. A report of the review must be presented to Parliament.

SECTION: 18

HEADING: Regulations

The Governor-General may, by Order in Council, make regulations for the purpose of giving full effect to this Act.
