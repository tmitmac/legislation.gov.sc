TITLE: Westland (Tierras del oeste) Independence Referendum Order 2026

ACTNUMBER: LI 2026/33

TYPE: Secondary

STATUS: In force

VERSION: Latest

VERSIONTITLE: (as made)

AGENCY: Ministry of Justice & Corrections

PARTNUMBER: 1

PARTTITLE: Order

SECTION: 1

HEADING: Title

This Order is the **Westland (Tierras del oeste) Independence Referendum Order 2026**.

SECTION: 2

HEADING: Commencement

This Order comes into force the day it receives royal assent.

SECTION: 3

HEADING: Declaration of referendum for purposes of Referendums Act 2026

A referendum on the question set out in [[section:4]] is a referendum for the purposes of the [[Act:Referendums Act 2026]].

SECTION: 4

HEADING: Referendum only to take place in Westland (Tierras del oeste)

For the purposes of conducting this referendum, only electors registered in Westland (Tierras del oeste) shall vote.

SECTION: 5

HEADING: Wording of first question to be put to electors

The wording of the first question to be put to electors in the referendum is—

CENTER: **"Should Westland (Tierras del oeste) be an independent country?"**

SECTION: 6

HEADING: Wording of options for first question which electors may vote

The wording of the 2 options for the second question which electors may vote in response to the question is—

CENTER: **Yes, Westland (Tierras del oeste) should be an independent country.**

CENTER: **No, Westland (Tierras del oeste) should not be an independent country.**

SECTION: 7

HEADING: Wording of second question to be put to electors

The wording of the second question to be put to electors in the referendum is—

CENTER: **"If Westland (Tierras del oeste) were to be an independent country, which system of government should it have?"**

SECTION: 8

HEADING: Wording of options for second question which electors may vote

CENTER:  **"I would choose a Parliamentary republic."**

CENTER:  **"I would choose a Presidential republic."**

CENTER:  **"I would choose a Constitutional monarchy."**

CENTER:  **"I would choose a Semi-presidential republic."**
