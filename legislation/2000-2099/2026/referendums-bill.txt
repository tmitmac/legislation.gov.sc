SECTION: 1

HEADING: Short Title

This Act is the **Referendums Act 2026**.

SECTION: 2

HEADING: Commencement

This Act comes into force the day after it receives Royal assent.

SECTION: 3

HEADING: Purpose

The purpose of this act is to—
(a) repeal Part 15 (Referendums) of the [[Act:Electoral Act 1986]];
(b) provide for clear and consistent law in regards to the conduct of referendums in Square Country.

SECTION: 4

HEADING: Act binds the Crown

This Act binds the Crown.

SECTION: 5

HEADING: Interpretation

1. In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—
**Act** means the [[Act:Electoral Act 1986]].
**advance vote** means a vote cast before polling day in accordance with Part 10 of the Act.


SECTION: 6

HEADING: Applications of this Act

1. This Act shall apply within all Square Country territory.
2. This Act applies to all referendums held after its commencement.
