TITLE: Electoral Amendment Act 2026

ACTNUMBER: 2026 No 07

TYPE: Act

STATUS: In force

VERSION: Latest

VERSIONTITLE: as at 19 October 2026

AGENCY: Ministry of Justice & Corrections

DATEOFASSENT: 19 October 2026

COMMENCEMENT: see section 2

PARTNUMBER: 1

PARTTITLE: Preliminary Provisions

SECTION: 1

HEADING: Title

This Act is the **Electoral Amendment Act 2026**

SECTION: 2

HEADING: Commencement

This Act comes into force immediately after it receives Royal assent.

SECTION: 3

HEADING: Principle Act

This Act amends the [[Act:Electoral Act 1986]].

PARTNUMBER: 2

PARTTITLE: Composition of Parliament

SECTION: 4

HEADING: Section 3 amended (Purpose)

In section 3(b), replace “13 electorate seats and 13 list seats” with “66 electorate seats and 30 list seats”.

SECTION: 5

HEADING: Section 78 amended (Number of electoral districts)

Replace section 78(1) with:

INSERT:
(1) Square Country shall be divided into 66 electoral districts.
ENDINSERT

SECTION: 6

HEADING: Section 81 amended (Functions of Boundary Commission)

Replace section 81(a) with:

INSERT:
(a) to divide Square Country into 66 electoral districts;
ENDINSERT

SECTION: 7

HEADING: Section 83A amended (Calculation of population quota)

In section 83A(b), replace “13” with “66”.

PARTNUMBER: 2

PARTTITLE: Allocation of seats

SECTION: 8

HEADING: Section 197 amended (Total number of seats)

Replace section 197(1) with:

INSERT:
(1) Parliament consists of—
(a) 64 electorate seats; and
(b) 30 list seats.
for a total of 96 seats.
(2) Parliament may temporarily exceed 96 seats if an overhang arises under this part.
ENDINSERT

SECTION: 9

HEADING: Section 202 amended (Allocation of seats to parties)

In section 202(1)(b), replace “26 seats” with “96 seats”.

SECTION: 10

HEADING: Section 207 amended (Effect of overhang on Parliament)

In section 207(1)(a), replace “26” with “96”.

PARTNUMBER: 3

PARTTITLE: Consequential amendments

SECTION: 11

HEADING: Schedule 1 amended (Sainte-Laguë method)

In clause 5(2), replace "26" with “96”, wherever it appears.

SECTION: 12

HEADING: Transitional arrangements

1. The Electoral Commission must conduct a review of electoral boundaries to establish 66 electoral districts.
2. The new electoral boundaries must take effect at the first general election held after this Act comes into force.
3. The existing composition of Parliament remains unchanged until that general election.

PARTNUMBER: 4

PARTTITLE: Final provision

SECTION: 13

HEADING: Savings

Except as expressly amended by this Act, the Electoral Act 1986 continues in force.
