SECTION: 4

HEADING: Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

**Act** means this Act and includes any amendment, replacement, or consolidation of this Act.
**business** means any person, company, partnership, organisation, undertaking, or entity engaged in commercial, industrial, professional, or other economic activity.
**Chief Executive** means the chief executive officer of the Ministry responsible for the administration of this Act.
**closed day** means a day on which trading activity is prohibited or restricted under this Act.
**contract of employment** means an agreement, whether written, oral, or implied, under which a person agrees to perform work or provide services for an employer in exchange for remuneration.

**government agency** means any department, ministry, authority, commission, office, or other body established by or under the laws of Square Country.
**holiday entitlement** means any right, benefit, payment, leave, or other entitlement arising from a public holiday under this Act.
**inspector** means a person appointed or authorised under this Act to monitor compliance with this Act and investigate suspected breaches.
**local authority** means a municipal council, regional authority, or other local government body established under the laws of Square Country.

**prescribed** means prescribed by regulations made under this Act.

**registered business** means a business registered or recognised under the laws of Square Country.
**regulations** means regulations made under this Act.
**retail trading** means the sale or supply of goods or services directly to consumers or members of the public.
**trading activity** includes the sale of goods or services by retail or wholesale.
**trading hours** means the period during which a business or person may lawfully conduct trading activity.
**wholesale trading** means the sale, supply, or distribution of goods or services to businesses, organisations, or other commercial entities rather than directly to consumers.
**worker** means an employee, contractor, self-employed person, or any other person who performs work or provides services.

SECTION: 4A

HEADING: Meaning of essential service

1. In this Act, **"essential service"** means any service—
(a) that is necessary for the protection of life, health, or public safety; or
(b) that is required to maintain the security or defence of Square Country; or
(c) that is required to maintain the continuous operation of critical national infrastructure; or
(d) that is prescribed, for the purposes of this Act, by regulations made under section XX.
2. Without limiting subsection (1), essential services include—
(a) **emergency services**, including police, fire, civil defence, and ambulance services;
(b) **health services**, including hospitals, urgent care, and clinical support services;
(c) **public utilities**, including electricity, water, wastewater, and telecommunications;
(d) **transport and navigation services** necessary for public safety or continuity of supply;
(e) any other service prescribed by regulation.

PARTNUMBER: 2

PARTTITLE: Public Holidays

SECTION: 7

HEADING: National Public Holidays

1. The following days are **national public holidays** throughout Square Country:
(a) New Year's Day — 1 January
(b) Day After New Years Day — 2 January
(c) Spring Holiday — First Monday of April
(d) Earlington Day — 17 April
(e) Summer Holiday — First Monday of July
(f) Sovereignty Day — 12 August
(g) Civic Day — 3 September
(h) Labour Day — Fourth Monday of October
(i) Rememberance Day — 11 November
(j) Christmas Eve — 24 December
(k) Christmas Day — 25 December
(l) Boxing Day — 26 December
2. If any national public holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, section 8 applies.

SECTION: 8

HEADING: Regional Public Holidays

1. The Minister may, by notice in the Gazette, declare regional public holidays for any Province or Territory.
2. A regional public holiday may be declared for—
(a) provincial anniversaries;
(b) significant cultural or historical events;
(c) agricultural, maritime, or industrial festivals of regional importance.
3. A notice under this section must specify the region, the date, and the reason for the declaration.

SECTION: 9

HEADING: Special Observance Days

1. The following days are special observance days, but are not public holidays unless declared under section 9:
(a) National Youth Day — 15 February
(b) Heritage Day — 19 October
2. Employers are encouraged to recognise these days, but no statutory entitlements arise unless otherwise provided.
