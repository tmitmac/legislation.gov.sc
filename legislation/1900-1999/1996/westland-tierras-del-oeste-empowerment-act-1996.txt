TITLE: Westland (Tierras del oeste) Empowerment Act 1996

ACTNUMBER: 1996 No 108

TYPE: Act

STATUS: In force

VERSION: Latest

VERSIONTITLE: as at 04 August 2026

AGENCY: the Ministry of Justice & Corrections

DATEOFASSENT: 1 January 1997

COMMENCEMENT: see section 1(2)

PARTNUMBER: 1

PARTTITLE: Preliminary Provisions

SECTION: 1

HEADING: Short Title and Commencement

1. This Act may be cited as the **Westland (Tierras del oeste) Empowerment Act 1996**.
2. This Act comes into force on 1 August 1996.
3. Different provisions of this Act may be brought into force on different dates by Order in Council, if so provided.

SECTION: 2

HEADING: Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—
**"Citizen"** means any citizen of Square Country or Ruby Country born Square Citizens who primarily resides in Westland (Tierras del oeste).
**“General Election”** means an election held for all seats in Parliament.
**"Insignificant laws"** has the meaning given to it in [[section:2AA]].
**"MP"** means a Member of His Majesty's Parliament of Square Country.
**“Party vote”** means the vote cast by an elector for a political party for the purpose of determining the allocation of list seats.
**"Significant laws"** has the meaning given to it in [[section:2AAA]].
**"Parliament"** means His Majesty's Parliament of Square Country.
**"Westland (Tierras del oeste)"** has the meaning given to it in [[section:2A]].

SECTION: 2A

HEADING: Meaning of Westland (Tierras del oeste)

Westland (Tierras del oeste) is the extraterritorial region of Square Country established under the [[Act:Westland Administrative Region Act 1946]].

SECTION: 2AA

HEADING: Meaning of insignificant laws

In this Act, insignificant laws means laws that do not have a reasonably major effect on the lives of citizens, and may only affect daily administrative operations 

SECTION: 2AB

HEADING: Meaning of significant laws

In this Act, significant laws means laws that have a major impact on the lives of citizens, and may affect constitutional issues.

SECTION: 3

HEADING: Purpose

The purpose of this Act is to—
(a) provide for the semi-independence of the Westland (Tierras del oeste) region;
(b) ensure the majority of decision-making power lies with the Westland (Tierras del oeste) Council established in [[section:6]]; and
(c) ensure robust administrative oversight of citizens and legislation.


SECTION: 4

HEADING: Act binds the Crown

This Act binds the Crown in right of Square Country.

SECTION: 5

HEADING: Relationship with other enactments

If any provision of this Act conflicts with another enactment, this Act prevails unless the other enactment expressly states otherwise.

PARTNUMBER: 2

PARTTITLE: Westland (Tierras del oeste) Council

SECTION: 6

HEADING: Establishment of the Westland (Tierras del oeste) Council

1. There is hereby an administrative body to be known as the Westland (Tierras del oeste) Council, hereinafter referred to as the **Council**.
2. The Council—
(a) is a body corporate with perpetual succession; and
(b) has a common seal; and
(c) may sue and be sued in its corporate name; and
(d) may acquire, hold, and dispose of property, and
(e) may do all things necessary or convenient to be done for or in connection with the performance of its functions.
 
SUBPARTNUMBER: 1

SUBPARTTITLE: Powers

SECTION: 7

HEADING: Power to make insignificant laws

1. At all times, the Council has the power to make insignificant laws in a way the Council best sees fit to serve the citizens of Westland (Tierras del oeste).
2. Once passed, these laws are final and cannot be altered or affected by Parliament.

SECTION: 8

HEADING: Limited power to make significant laws

1. At all times, the Council has the limited power to make significant laws.
2. In the interest of consistency, alterations to significant laws may only be performed with the prior consent of Parliament.
3. Parliament retains the rights to legislate for education, health, and justice.

SECTION: 9

HEADING: Power to call for independence referendum

1. The Council has the right to call for a referendum on the independence of Westland (Tierras del oeste).
2. Full independence cannot be granted without the democratic acceptance of the affirmative.
3. If the referendum passes, Parliament cannot appeal.
4. All referendums must strictly adhere to the [[Act:Referendums Act 2026]].
5. [*Repealed*]

AMENDMENT: Section 9(5): repealed, on 4 August 2026, by section 4 of the Westland (Tierras del oeste) Amendment Act 2026 (2026 No 04).
AMENDMENT: Section 9(4): replaced, on 26 August 2026, by schedule 1 of the Referendums Act 2026 (2026 No 05).


SECTION: 9A

HEADING: Declaration of referendum

1. If, under [[section:9(1)]], the Council calls for a referendum, the Council must provide a **Declaration of Referendum Intentions** to Parliament.
2. This Declaration must include—
(a) the referendum question;
(b) the proposed timeline of the referendum; and
(c) indication of whether the referendum is indicative or binding.
3. Parliament may not reject, decline, or disregard, to any effect, a declaration made by the Council.

AMENDMENT: Section 9A: inserted, on 4 August 2026, by section 5 of the Westland (Tierras del oeste) Amendment Act 2026 (2026 No 04).

SUBPARTNUMBER: 2

SUBPARTTITLE: Council Structure

SECTION: 10

HEADING: Council to determine structure

1. The Council has the power to determine its own structure with the assumption that—
(a) the Council provides for reasonable representation of citizens;
(b) the Council reasonably maintains democratic values; and
(c) the Council acts in good faith and without corruption.
2. Without limiting subsection (1), Parliament retains the power to interfere if the Council does not uphold its basic values set out in subsection (1).

PARTNUMBER: 3

PARTTITLE: Status of Westland (Tierras del oeste)

SECTION: 11

HEADING: Recognition of Westland (Tierras del oeste)

Westland (Tierras del oeste) is hereby recognised by Parliament as a **semi-independent territory**.

SUBPARTNUMBER: 1

SUBPARTTITLE: Obligations of Parliament

SECTION: 12

HEADING: Financial obligations of Parliament

1. Parliament is obliged to fund basic infrastructure, including but not limited to—
(a) roads, bridges, and tunnels;
(b) energy & power;
(c) water & sanitation; and
(d) telecommunications.
2. The Council may appeal to Parliament if infrastructure is not adequately funded.

SECTION: 13

HEADING: Security obligations of Parliament

1. Parliament is obliged to provide a security guarantee to Westland (Tierras del oeste) in the potential event of any land, sea, or air invasion.
2. Parliament is obliged to allocate a reasonable portion of funding to military advancements in Westland (Tierras del oeste).

SECTION: 14

HEADING: Moral obligations of Parliament

1. Parliament is morally obliged to ensure the stability of regional governance in Westland (Tierras del oeste).
2. Parliament is morally obliged to assist in the resolution of any social disputes should the Council require it.

PARTNUMBER: 4

PARTTITLE: Electoral Matters

SECTION: 15

HEADING: Participation in General Elections

1. At each general election, the Council has the power to determine whether participation shall take place.
2. The Council can determine participation in any way it sees fit.
3. Parliament may not force the Council to take part.
4. Without limiting subsection (1), the Council must adhere to the [[Act:Electoral Act 1986]] at all times should it decide to take part.

SECTION: 16

HEADING: Westland (Tierras del oeste) not to return any electorate MPs

1. In any general election, there shall be no electorate MPs representing Westland (Tierras del oeste).
2. Subsection (1) does not limit the Council's ability to take part in a general election under [[section:15]].

PARTNUMBER: 5

PARTTITLE: Relationship with Square Country

SECTION: 17

HEADING: Cooperation

Parliament will always cooperate fully, entirely, and in good faith with the Council.

SECTION: 18

HEADING: Dispute Resolution

Parliament will do the utmost to resolve any dispute that arises between Parliament and the Council.

PARTNUMBER: 6

PARTTITLE: Miscellaneous

SECTION: 19

HEADING: Review

1. Parliament must commission a review into this Act five years after it comes into force.
2. The report must include its—
(a) effectiveness;
(b) socioeconomic results; and
(c) any recommended amendments.

SECTION: 20

HEADING: Transitional Provisions

1. Any insignificant bill related to Westland (Tierras del oeste) in Parliament before the commencement of this Act shall hereby be terminated.
2. Any significant bill related to Westland (Tierras del oeste) in Parliament before the commencement of this Act shall continue to progress through the legislative system.

SECTION: 21

HEADING: Repeals

The following Acts are hereby repealed—
(a) [[Act:Westland Administrative Region Act 1946]];
(b) any other enactment inconsistent with this Act.
