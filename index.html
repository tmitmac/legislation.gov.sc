About Square Country Legislation

Legislation published on this portal is the official law of Square Country. Acts are passed by Parliament and receive Royal Assent before becoming law.

This website provides access to current legislation, historical legislation, Bills and legislative information. It is maintained by the Office of Legislative Counsel.
